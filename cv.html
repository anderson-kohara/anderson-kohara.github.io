01 / Research

Research focus

I introduced a new equation based on Regge field theory to describe the energy evolution of complex elastic scattering amplitudes in the semiclassical regime.

Predicting cross sections at other energies

The equation uses phenomenological elastic scattering models as boundary conditions to predict cross sections at other energies. A single parameter governs the energy evolution of the Pomeron.

Connection with small-x physics

In the semiclassical approximation, the equation has a structure analogous to the Balitsky–Kovchegov (BK) equation. Beyond this approximation, the exact formulation leads to a system of coupled equations with a structure reminiscent of JIMWLK evolution.

Work in progress · First-principles derivation

I am preparing an article aimed at deriving this equation from first principles using statistical mechanics and a microscopic description.

Other research experience and methods
  • Modeling complex dynamical processes in strong interactions and high energy particle scattering.
  • Statistical analysis of accelerator data from LHC/CERN, Tevatron/Fermilab, and STAR/RHIC.
  • Analysis of proton–air collisions in cosmic ray showers.
  • Analytical models for elementary particle scattering at high energies.
  • Monte Carlo simulation of diffractive strong interactions based on QCD.
  • Mathematical methods for derivative dispersion and Kramers–Kronig relations.
